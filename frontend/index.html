How it works

From a single leaf to a connected community

Every report moves through the same transparent loop, and every step is recorded.

01

Report

The farmer sends a Kannada or Hindi voice note and a leaf photo on Telegram. No app to install.

02

Assess

AI gives a preliminary result with a confidence band, or asks for a clearer photo when it is unsure.

03

Remember

The report joins the farm’s timeline together with the weather context at that moment.

04

Escalate

Rules, not the AI, send unclear or concerning cases to an agricultural expert with full context.

05

Warn

Similar nearby reports can form a potential cluster and trigger a cautious early-warning message.

06

Follow up

Reminders collect new observations over time, so the farm’s story keeps growing.

Preliminary assessment. Not a confirmed diagnosis. Confidence is reported by the model and is not calibrated.
Honest by design

AI that knows when to stay quiet

KhetLoop supports experts instead of replacing them, and it shows its reasoning on every report.

  • Abstains when the evidence is weak and asks for a clearer photo.
  • Rules decide when to escalate or flag a potential cluster, not the language model.
  • Never prescribes pesticide names or dosages.
  • The status changes to Expert Reviewed only when a human replies.
  • Every report explains itself with “Why am I seeing this?”.
